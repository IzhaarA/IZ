# TikTok promotion plan: videos made 22 Oct 2026

Budgets below are an example of **R100 a day in total**. If you spend more or less, keep the same split.
Pick "Messages" (WhatsApp/profile) or "Website visits" as the goal, so you can see which video brings orders.
Province targeting may need TikTok Ads Manager (ads.tiktok.com); the in-app "Promote" button may only allow the whole country.

## Two-week schedule

**Week 1 (test), R20 a day each, 7 days:**
Ndebele Accessories · Mink Blankets · Czech Seed Beads · Chinese Seed Beads · Bangles

**Week 2, R20 a day each, 7 days:**
Cotton & Twine · Plastic Beads 6mm · Towels, plus the 2 best videos from week 1

**Post without promoting:** Plastic Beads 3mm and 8mm Oval (promote later if they do well on their own).

## How to judge each video
- Count the WhatsApp messages and orders that mention the video, not just views.
- After 3–4 days, stop any video that has many views but no messages, and move its budget to the best one.
- Keep promoting the winners for longer, and make new ads in the same style for those categories.
